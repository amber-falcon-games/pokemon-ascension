# Vision

Pokémon Ascension: an authentic Emerald-era handheld game whose battles feel like the anime — creative, dynamic, strategic and dramatic without excessive complexity. Player remains the trainer; no autobattler, no grid positioning, no MMO UI. Mobile emulator usability matters. Preserve recognisable pixel-art presentation and concise default UI; optional detail on demand.

Success is winning through clever preparation and adaptation, not merely level advantage. Wild battles should remain quick; trainer and boss battles should afford multiple exchanges and counterplay. Ordinary OHKOs and effortless sweeping should be discouraged; exceptional moves or deliberate setups may permit them.

Long-term: expanded evolution, permanent Megas, move mastery/branching, accessible knowledge, less grinding, exploration, secrets, optional bosses and interconnected mythology. First prove one compelling tactical battle in the Lab before investing in new campaign content. Keep the campaign playable after baseline. Private fan project; review IP constraints before distribution.
